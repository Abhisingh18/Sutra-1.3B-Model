Inside the MoE layers 1–15

A router scores 48 experts and keeps the best four. One shared expert always runs. The other 44 are never touched for that token.
4 routed 44 idle 1 shared, always on

Sigmoid scoring with bias-based balancing — overloaded experts get nudged down, idle ones up, outside the loss. Measured across the whole run: load 1.9–2.4% against 2.1% uniform, zero dead experts.

Inside the attention

Keys and values are squeezed through a 256-wide latent, so the cache holds 288 numbers per token instead of 2,048.

nope — 64 dims rides through the latent, carries content
rope — 32 dims bypasses compression, carries position

There is no positional encoding at the input. RoPE rotates Q and K inside attention, in every layer — so what survives the dot product is relative distance, not absolute position.
